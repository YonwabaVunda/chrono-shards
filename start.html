The Ruins of Celestia’s Castle

You awaken in a colossal fortress suspended between earth and sky, its marble halls cracked and overgrown with glowing vines that still hum with Celestia’s fading life. Once the heart of the world’s balance, the castle now floats in fragments above a golden mist, echoing with the whispers of what was lost. Your task is to collect three golden Celestia's coin, each holding a memory shard that restores part of the world. The first, the Coin of Growth, lies in the overgrown courtyard guarded by living vines, symbolizing renewal; collecting it makes flowers bloom and Celestia’s faint voice stir. The second, the Coin of Memory, rests within the echoing hall where ghosts of the past replay fragments of life before the fracture, revealing a glimpse of the world that once was. The final coin, the Coin of Balance, is hidden in the fallen throne room.Once all three are gathered, the coins fuse into the First Shard of Celestia, radiating light that revives part of the land. As sunlight pierces through the ruins, Celestia’s voice calls out, “My child of shards… the first breath returns. Seek the others before the void consumes all,” as a bridge forms leading to the next broken realm.
